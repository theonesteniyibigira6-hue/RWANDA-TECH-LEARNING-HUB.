FAQ

Sponsorship questions

Can someone sponsor the entire RTLH website?

Yes. The Whole-Website Sponsor option supports the broader RTLH ecosystem, including development, infrastructure, content, security, operations and future features. Scope and conditions should be documented in a customised agreement.

Can I sponsor only the E-Teacher?

Yes. Sponsors can request E-Teacher, E-Class, E-Library, courses, scholarships, digital skills, career initiatives or another agreed programme.

Do I have to contribute money?

Not necessarily. Depending on the project, support may include funding, technology, software, content, expertise, equipment, training, scholarships or other valuable resources.

Will sponsorship give me control of RTLH?

No. Sponsorship does not automatically grant ownership or control. Any special rights must be explicitly agreed in writing.

How is application information handled?

It should be processed only for stated purposes with appropriate safeguards. Rwanda's data-protection framework requires informed consent to be specific, informed and unambiguous where consent is the basis for processing, and provides rights such as withdrawal, access, rectification and erasure in applicable circumstances.
