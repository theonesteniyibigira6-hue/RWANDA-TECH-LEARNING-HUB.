Why this platform exists

Turning educational challenges into digital opportunities

Education should not be limited by distance, time, lack of resources or the cost of travelling to a physical classroom.

📚

Limited access to resources

Many learners still face unequal access to quality books, teachers, digital materials and specialised learning.

Our platform brings courses, an e-library, structured lessons and digital learning resources together so learners can keep learning beyond the traditional classroom.
Read more
⏱️

Time and distance barriers

Travel, schedules and geographical distance can make regular learning difficult for students, teachers and working people.

Online courses, e-teacher services and e-classes can reduce unnecessary travel and allow learning to fit around real-life responsibilities.
Read more
💡

Digital-skills gap

The world is changing quickly, creating a growing need for technology, creativity, entrepreneurship and future-ready skills.

RTLH is built to connect education with practical digital skills and opportunities, helping learners become creators, problem-solvers and contributors.
Read more

Who we are

Our Vision & Our Mission

🌅

Our Vision

To become a trusted, inclusive and innovative digital learning ecosystem that helps Rwanda and the wider world access knowledge, build skills, create solutions and participate confidently in the digital future.

We envision a future where a learner can discover a course, access a resource, meet a teacher, join a digital class, build a project and connect with opportunities regardless of location or background.
Read more
🎯

Our Mission

To empower learners, teachers, schools and organisations through accessible online courses, e-library resources, e-teacher services, e-classes, mentorship, opportunities and technology-driven learning experiences.

We aim to support lifelong learning, digital literacy, creativity, entrepreneurship and practical skills while creating meaningful connections between education and the opportunities of the modern world.
