Partnership guidelines

Our standards for responsible collaboration

Every RTLH partnership should protect learners, respect professional standards and create genuine educational value.

🌱 OUR PRINCIPLE Education First. Integrity Always. Impact Together.

RTLH seeks partnerships based on trust, transparency, innovation, inclusion, professionalism, mutual benefit, learner protection and long-term impact.

✓
Commitment to education
Partners should share RTLH's commitment to education, learning and positive social impact.
✓
Accuracy and quality
Partners must provide accurate, appropriate and useful information, resources or services.
✓
Respect and inclusion
Partners must respect learners, educators, staff and other partners and avoid discriminatory or harmful conduct.
✓
Legal and ethical compliance
All partners must follow applicable laws, regulations and relevant professional standards.
✓
Privacy and learner protection
Partners must protect learner privacy and personal information and use data responsibly.
✓
Professional communication
Partners should communicate professionally and honour agreed responsibilities, deliverables and deadlines.
✓
Intellectual property
Partners must respect copyright and intellectual-property rights. Ownership and licensing of contributed content should be agreed before publication.
✓
RTLH brand protection
Partners must not use the RTLH name, logo or branding without appropriate permission.
✓
Transparency
Commercial relationships, sponsorships and financial arrangements should be clearly disclosed and documented where appropriate.

How it works

From idea to partnership

Our process is designed to make collaboration clear, professional and focused on impact.

1

Submit Request

Complete the Partnership Application Form and tell us about your organisation, idea and proposed contribution.

2

Initial Review

RTLH reviews the proposal, objectives, suitability and potential educational value.

3

Discussion

If appropriate, RTLH contacts the applicant to discuss the proposal, expectations and opportunities.

4

Proposal

Both parties agree on responsibilities, resources, objectives and expected outcomes.

5

Approval

RTLH formally approves the partnership after the relevant review process.

6

Agreement

Where necessary, both parties sign a partnership agreement or Memorandum of Understanding.

7

Launch

The partnership begins according to the agreed plan, responsibilities and timeline.

8

Review & Impact

The partnership may be periodically reviewed to measure results and improve collaboration.

Frequently asked questions

Partnership FAQ

Who can apply to partner with RTLH?

Schools, universities, companies, technology organisations, NGOs, public institutions, educators, content providers, sponsors, employers, professionals and individuals with a meaningful contribution can apply.

Does submitting the form guarantee a partnership?

No. Submission begins the review process. RTLH may accept, request clarification on, or decline a proposal based on suitability, capacity, safety, legal and educational considerations.

Can a partnership be customised?

Yes. Partnership responsibilities, resources, objectives, duration, benefits and expected outcomes can be discussed and documented according to the nature of the collaboration.

Can international organisations partner with RTLH?

Yes. RTLH's vision includes serving learners in Rwanda and around the world, and suitable international collaborations are welcome.

Can I contribute courses, books or educational videos?

Yes. Content partnerships can include books, videos, courses, tutorials, articles, research and other educational resources, subject to quality, copyright, licensing and platform requirements.

Can companies support scholarships or internships?

Yes. Scholarship, employment and career partnerships can support learners through sponsored learning, internships, jobs, career development and industry training.

Will RTLH publish every partner's information?

No. Public recognition and the use of partner information should be appropriate to the partnership and agreed where necessary. Personal information should be handled responsibly.
