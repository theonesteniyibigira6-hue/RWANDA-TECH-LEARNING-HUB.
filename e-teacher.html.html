RTLH E-Teacher professional programme

Core teaching capabilities

Online coaching, training, course delivery, e-class formation, lesson management, homework, assignments, projects, quizzes, tests, exams, automated assessment windows, learner results, enrollment, attendance, live meetings, posts, announcements and communication.

Business & trust capabilities

Public teacher directory, public class catalogue, teacher verification, student payments, configurable RTLH fees/withholding, earnings, payout requests, reviews, analytics and certificate workflows.

Future integrations

Email provider, payment gateway, live-stream provider, cloud video storage, calendar, push notifications, AI teaching assistant and AI learner assistant can be connected through secure backend services.

Scale principle

Firestore stores structured metadata and permissions. Large media/video delivery should use appropriate storage/streaming infrastructure. Sensitive payment and administrative actions should run server-side.
