Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Rwanda Tech Learning Hub is committed to protecting your privacy and transparently handling your personal data[cite: 3]. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials upon account creation.
  • Learning Progress: Course completions, quiz scores, book reading logs, and generated certificates.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information gathered automatically via secure authentication and hosting services (such as Firebase/Supabase).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Provide access to learning modules, quizzes, and digital library resources.

3. Data Protection & Storage

Your data is securely stored using industry-standard encrypted backend services. We implement security rules to ensure that only authenticated users can access or update their personal account data[cite: 3].

4. Managing and Deleting Your Data

You retain full control over your personal information[cite: 3]. You may review, update, or request the deletion of your account and personal data at any time through your account settings on the website[cite: 3].

5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We utilize secure backend infrastructure providers strictly for database hosting, authentication, and platform analytics.

6. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to manage your personal data, please contact us through the platform dashboard[cite: 3].
